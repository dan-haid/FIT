## 4. Key Takeaways & Consistency Strategy

1. **Gym Consistency (Closing the Gap):**
   * Target is 5 days/week, currently hitting 3–4 days. 
   * **Rule:** If busy, aim for short 45-minute sessions instead of skipping completely. 3-4 days is still great progress for beginner body recomposition—consistency over perfection!

2. **German Supermarket High-Protein Staples (REWE / Lidl / Aldi):**
   * **Dairy:** *Magerquark*, *Skyr*, *Körniger Frischkäse* (Low-fat cottage cheese), Light Halloumi/Grillkäse.
   * **Meat & Fish:** *Hähnchenbrust* (Chicken breast), *Putenbrust* (Turkey), 5% Fat *Mageres Rinderhackfleisch* (Lean beef mince).
   * **Cheese:** *Hartkäse* (Parmesan/Grana Padano) – dense in protein (~30-38g/100g); best finely grated for flavor without excess calories.

3. **Tracking Tip:**
   * High-carb days (like *daal chawal* or *börek*) cause temporary water retention due to glycogen and sodium storage. Weight spikes on the scale the next morning are water, not body fat!
